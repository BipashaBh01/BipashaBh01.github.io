About

I have worked in building machine learning and deep learning models. With five years of software engineering experience and recent advanced work in PyTorch-based deep learning and multimodal computer vision, including developing an end-to-end face anti-spoofing system, I have built a strong foundation in designing, training, evaluating, and debugging complex neural-network pipelines. I am excited for roles like this, which sits at the intersection of research, machine learning and practical software development with an impact, which is exactly where I see my strengths developing.

I bring 5 years of software engineering experience in financial services, working on complex projects for a leading global bank, together with research-driven AI experience gained through an MSc in Data Science and Advanced Computing at the University of Reading. I was awarded the highly competitive, fully funded Felix Scholarship (£52,612) for undertaking the MSc course. I have worked in agile environments, collaborated closely with stakeholders and cross-functional teams, and taken on leadership responsibilities.

My most substantial recent work is an end-to-end face anti-spoofing and liveness detection system combining a passive deep learning model with an active verification layer that prompts users to perform random live actions. I designed a multimodal architecture using visual, frequency and motion information, trained it on HPC GPUs, and achieved 98.82% AUC and a 97.8% attack-detection rate, including evaluation on unseen attack conditions. I also built the complete data pipeline and integrated the system into a real-time web application using FastAPI, WebSockets and Uvicorn.

My wider project experience includes an Azure predictive modelling pipeline combining more than two million flight records with large-scale weather data, aerial object detection using satellite imagery, and heart-disease prediction and severity classification. These projects gave me experience across structured and unstructured data, feature engineering, model training, evaluation and working across different problem domains.

Prior to my MSc, I spent 5 years in software quality engineering and automation, working across the full testing lifecycle from requirements analysis and planning through execution and closure. I ensured top-tier software quality, bringing down defect rejection and leakage, and reducing manual works by optimising efficiency through automation. I received customer satisfaction reviews, along with two promotions and an award for sustained high performance and professional excellence.

I also participated in an examination-based certification course called ‘Artificial Intelligence – Constraint Satisfaction Problems’, conducted by the NPTEL India, during my undergraduate studies in 2018. This was my earliest formal exposure to AI, and I was recognised as the national topper for my performance.

I am keen to bring my software engineering background and growing AI expertise into roles where I can help build practical, reliable, and meaningful solutions.
